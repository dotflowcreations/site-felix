Laboratory Compliance

Analytical & Research Use Only. All documented reference standards are intended exclusively for non-clinical laboratory analysis, method calibration, and in-vitro scientific testing. Not for human, veterinary, therapeutic, or food use.

Scope of Supply & Laboratory Intended Use

All reference materials and analytical compounds cataloged by Felix Chemical Supply are distributed strictly for in-vitro laboratory research, chromatographic calibration, and scientific assay development. These materials should only be handled by trained laboratory personnel in accordance with established chemical hygiene and laboratory safety procedures.

Materials cataloged in this repository are not registered as pharmaceuticals, cosmetics, dietary supplements, agricultural agents, or household chemicals. They are not intended, formulated, or authorized for any in-vivo application, clinical diagnostics, medical treatment, or administration to humans or animals.

Research chemical standards are designated exclusively for non-clinical analytical investigation, conforming to regulatory standards for laboratory research materials. Purchasing institutions and researchers are responsible for confirming compliance with their institutional oversight policies, facility safety guidelines, and relevant regional regulatory frameworks.

Safe Handling & Chain of Custody

Recipients must ensure appropriate storage conditions, including refrigeration or desiccation as specified on analytical data sheets, to preserve molecular stability for accurate spectroscopic and chromatographic measurement.
