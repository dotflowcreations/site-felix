Terms of Research Supply

All products supplied by Felix Chemical Supply (operating under Chem-LLC) are manufactured and distributed exclusively for laboratory, scientific, and in-vitro research purposes where legally permissible under local, state, and federal law.

By placing an inquiry or acquiring materials, the purchaser expressly certifies that they are an authorized researcher, scientific investigator, or institutional representative operating standard laboratory safety containment. Products are strictly not intended for human consumption, medical diagnosis, therapeutic application, veterinary administration, or household consumer use.

The purchaser assumes all legal and regulatory responsibility for the handling, storage, possession, and hazardous disposal of all chemical reagents and lyophilized compounds in full adherence to applicable scientific protocols.

Privacy & Data Security Policy

Felix Chemical Supply adheres to strict institutional confidentiality standards. Customer contact details, inquiries, and logistics transmissions are used solely for order processing, carrier dispatch, and technical customer service.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or distribute customer information to third-party advertisers or marketing aggregators. Communication via our direct SMS channel or designated Telegram (@felixchemicalsupply) is handled with commercial discretion.

Payment settlement records utilizing cryptocurrency (USDT / USDC ERC20) remain decentralized and pseudonymized on the Ethereum blockchain, providing transaction finality without requiring invasive personal banking disclosures.

Domestic U.S. Shipping Policy

Orders dispatched from our domestic USA warehouse are processed in the order received, typically within 24–48 business hours. Carrier collection occurs Monday through Friday.

Domestic transit times typically range from 2 to 4 business days depending on delivery destination and carrier routing. All materials are packed in temperature-controlled, shock-absorbent packaging to maintain lyophilized stability during transit.

Tracking numbers are transmitted via SMS or Telegram immediately upon carrier handoff. Customers are responsible for verifying the accuracy of their physical delivery addresses prior to shipment dispatch.

Return & Replacement Policy

Due to the sensitive biochemical integrity of lyophilized peptides and laboratory reagents, returned products cannot be accepted back into our sterile storage facility once dispatched from our warehouse.

In the verified event of carrier transit damage, containment breach, or verifiable loss, contact Felix customer service within 48 hours of carrier notification. Please provide clear photographic documentation of the package condition and shipping label to initiate a replacement claim.

All claims are reviewed promptly by our fulfillment team to ensure resolution for legitimate research disruptions.
